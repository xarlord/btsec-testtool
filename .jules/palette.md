## 2025-02-07 - Add Keyboard Actions for Quick Submission
**Learning:** Adding `keyboardOptions` and `keyboardActions` allows users to submit forms directly via the keyboard, enhancing accessibility and flow, and ensuring users have the same interaction patterns they might expect from a form.
**Action:** When implementing forms in Jetpack Compose, use `keyboardOptions` and `keyboardActions` to ensure users can submit forms smoothly.
