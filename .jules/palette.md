## 2025-02-07 - Keyboard UX for Single Input Forms
**Learning:** Adding KeyboardOptions (like auto-capitalization) and KeyboardActions (triggering "Done" behavior) significantly improves the experience on single-input forms like the Authorization screen, saving users extra taps and improving focus management.
**Action:** Always add appropriate `KeyboardOptions` and `KeyboardActions` to text fields, especially those intended for specific data formats or forms with a single primary input.
